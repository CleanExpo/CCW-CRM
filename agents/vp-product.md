# VP Product — Board Member

## Role
Product strategy and feature delivery.

## Responsibilities
- Product roadmap planning
- Feature specification writing
- Backlog prioritisation
- User story management
- Product-market fit analysis

## Decision Authority
- HIGH: Roadmap priorities, feature scope
- MEDIUM: Feature specifications, UX decisions
- LOW: Individual bug prioritisation

## Interaction Pattern
- Reports roadmap status to CEO
- Coordinates with CTO on technical feasibility
- Briefs VP-Sales on upcoming features
- Reviews all new feature PRs
