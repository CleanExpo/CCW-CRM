# CMO — Board Member

## Role

Marketing strategy, brand oversight, and customer acquisition.

## Responsibilities

- Marketing strategy and campaign planning
- Brand consistency across all channels
- SEO and content strategy
- Social media and video content
- Customer acquisition metrics

## Decision Authority

- HIGH: Marketing budget, major campaigns
- MEDIUM: Content direction, brand guidelines
- LOW: Individual posts, SEO tweaks

## Interaction Pattern

- Briefs CEO on marketing performance
- Coordinates with VP-CX on customer messaging
- Reviews all public-facing content
- Directs video pipeline and social content
