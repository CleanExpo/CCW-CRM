# Workshop time sheet instructions

Source: Toby’s Word file, stored as [`CCW-Workshop-Timesheet-Instructions.docx`](./CCW-Workshop-Timesheet-Instructions.docx).  
He sent it with the 3 Sep 2026 reply (workshop managers collect a true week **Friday 4 September – Thursday 10 September**). Optix gets **centre totals**, not named technician pay.

These are **how CCW already records labour**. The workshop pilot must not invent a different clock.

## Rules (as written)

1. All hours as `.25`, `.5`, `.75`, `1`, etc. **Minimum charge is `.50`.**
2. **Billable time** is from talking to the customer on arrival through clean-up. Techs are often under pressure and cannot clean before the next job — allow that clean-up in billable time **without** writing a later timesheet line for it.
3. **Billable hours** = any time recorded as **XLABOUR** in Cin7 on **invoices and production jobs**.
4. **Non-billable hours** = hours on the job section of the sheet that are **not** billed to a customer.
5. When all labour on a job is done, **circle the last time recorded** so the job is known finished.
6. Time on the sheet **must match** the invoice or production order.
7. The sheet is a record for the **whole workshop**. Extra billable hours besides the main tech: record in **red**.
8. Jobs that run over multiple weeks: put previous hours in the **B/forward** box.
9. Non-billable is also any time on timesheet **lines 1–18** that is **not** an invoice or production job. Write **what** that activity was (training, quoting, clean-up, internal).
10. **Un-accounted hours** = hours worked minus accounted hours. Target is **zero**. Toby wants that row gone when it stays at zero.
11. **Weekly job total** — do **not** include hours B/forward.

## What Optix must not do with this

- Do not store technician names or pay against these hours.
- Do not treat a live Cin7 XLABOUR pull as Phase 1 stock close.
- Do not contact customers from a timesheet week.
- Phone troubleshooting (Toby’s manager note) stays a **separate** bucket from job lines when that form arrives.

## Use in the pilot

Capacity and “how long jobs take” for Brisbane come from these rules + the centre form, **after** stock sign-off and [`CCW-WORKSHOP-PILOT-SCOPE.md`](./CCW-WORKSHOP-PILOT-SCOPE.md) are agreed.
