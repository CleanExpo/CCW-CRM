## INTRO SECTION

**Heading**: Why CCT Certification Changes Your Business

The IICRC Carpet Cleaning Technician (CCT) certification isn't just a credential to hang on the wall. It's the foundational training that separates professional operators from the rest.

CCW Online is an authorised IICRC training provider delivering the CCT course in Brisbane for Queensland-based cleaning professionals.

**What you'll learn:**

- Carpet fibre identification and how different fibres respond to cleaning agents
- Soil and stain classification — treating the right problem the right way
- Chemistry of cleaning: pH, enzyme action, oxidation, and encapsulation
- Hot water extraction technique and equipment calibration
- Professional drying methods and damage prevention
- How to handle difficult situations: delicate fibres, set stains, client communication

## FAQ SECTION

**Heading**: Common Questions

**Do I need prior experience to attend?**
No prior experience required. The CCT is suitable for complete beginners as well as experienced operators who want formal recognition.

**Is the exam difficult?**
The exam is a written test at the end of the course. The study materials prepare you for it. Most participants who engage with the course material throughout pass first time.

**What does the certification cost?**
Call 1300 229 273 or email sales@ccwonline.com.au for current pricing including any group booking discounts.

**Can I bring my team?**
Yes — group bookings welcome. Contact us for group pricing for 3+ participants from the same company.

**What happens if I don't pass the exam?**
IICRC provides a re-examination process. Your trainer will advise on next steps.
