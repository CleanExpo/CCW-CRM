## Section 1: What Is a Truckmount Carpet Cleaning Machine?

A truckmount is a self-contained carpet cleaning system permanently mounted inside a van or truck. Unlike portable extractors that run off mains power and draw water from a sink, a truckmount:

- Generates its own heat (200°F+/93°C+) using the vehicle engine or an on-board motor
- Provides high-pressure water flow (typically 500–1,200 PSI)
- Produces strong continuous vacuum (water lift of 180–400 inches)
- Stores clean water and waste water in separate tanks inside the vehicle

The operator simply runs hoses from the van to the property. No extension cords. No waiting for a domestic hot water system. Just consistent professional-grade cleaning performance on every job.

**Why operators upgrade to truckmounts:**

- Higher productivity: clean 3–4 homes or 2–3 commercial jobs per day
- Better results: higher heat destroys bacteria and removes embedded soil that portables miss
- Client perception: arriving with a van-mounted system signals professionalism
- Lower operating cost per job vs. portable machines over 2+ years

## Section 2: Types of Truckmount Drive Systems

### 2.1 Direct Drive (Engine-Powered)

The most common setup in Australia. The van's engine powers the blower and pump through a belt/pulley system. Power is available as long as the engine runs.

**Pros:** Reliable, no secondary engine to maintain, lower upfront cost
**Cons:** Van must idle during cleaning (fuel cost, emissions)

### 2.2 Twin-Axle (Slide-In Units)

A self-contained unit on a slide-out frame. Popular for operators who rotate between multiple vans or don't want permanent installation.

**Pros:** Portable between vehicles, no modification to van required
**Cons:** Heavier, slightly less power than fully-mounted units

### 2.3 Gas-Powered (Independent Motor)

A separate petrol or LPG engine provides power, independent of the van's engine. Common in high-production commercial applications.

**Pros:** Can run in enclosed spaces (with ventilation), high output
**Cons:** More expensive, additional maintenance items

## Section 3: Key Specifications to Compare

When comparing truckmount carpet cleaning machines, focus on these four metrics:

| Spec                | Entry Level | Mid-Range | High Production |
| ------------------- | ----------- | --------- | --------------- |
| Water Lift (inches) | 180–220     | 240–300   | 330–400         |
| PSI (Pressure)      | 500–700     | 700–1,000 | 1,000–1,200     |
| Heat (°F)           | 180–200     | 200–230   | 210–240         |
| CFM (Airflow)       | 180–230     | 230–280   | 280–350         |

**Water lift** matters most for extraction — higher lift means more moisture removed, faster drying times, and less chance of mould growth in the carpet.

**PSI** matters for soil agitation and pre-spray activation. Too high on delicate fibres risks damage; too low on heavily soiled commercial carpet leaves residue.

**Heat** is critical for sanitisation and faster chemical activation. Consistently hitting 200°F+ is the standard for IICRC-certified work.

## Section 4: Cost to Buy and Set Up a Truckmount in Australia

### Machine Cost

- **Entry-level units** (e.g., small dual-wand): $12,000–$18,000 AUD
- **Mid-range production units**: $18,000–$28,000 AUD
- **High-production commercial units**: $28,000–$45,000+ AUD

### Van Preparation

Budget $3,000–$8,000 AUD for:

- Hose reels (solution and vacuum hoses, 50m each minimum)
- Water and waste tanks (typically 100L fresh / 150L waste)
- Van racking and equipment storage
- Electrical wiring for controls and lighting
- Graphics/branding (optional but professional)

### Ongoing Operating Costs

- Fuel (idling during cleaning): $15–$25 per day
- Chemicals (per room): $1.50–$4.00 depending on system
- Maintenance schedule: 500-hour service intervals
- Water (portable water tank fill): minimal ($0.50–$2.00 per job)

### Total First-Year Budget

Expect to invest $20,000–$38,000 AUD in your first truckmount setup when including machine, van prep, initial chemical stock, and IICRC training.

## Section 6: Truckmount Maintenance Schedule

To protect your investment, follow this maintenance schedule:

**After every use:**

- Flush the system with clean water (run 5 minutes after last job)
- Drain waste tank completely
- Check hose connections for wear

**Weekly:**

- Check engine oil and coolant levels
- Inspect belts and pulleys for wear
- Clean chemical metering jets

**Every 200 hours:**

- Change engine oil and filter
- Inspect vacuum blower for deposits
- Test chemical metering accuracy

**Every 500 hours:**

- Full service by qualified technician
- Replace belts if showing wear
- Calibrate pressure and heat settings

## FAQ Section (FAQPage JSON-LD)

**Q1: How long does a truckmount last?**
A well-maintained truckmount will last 8–15 years. Regular servicing at 500-hour intervals, prompt repairs, and proper winterisation (not relevant in most of Australia but important in alpine regions) are the main factors.

**Q2: Do I need a special van for a truckmount?**
Most high-roof cargo vans (Ford Transit, Mercedes Sprinter, Iveco Daily) are compatible. The key requirements are adequate load capacity (typically 300–600kg for the unit + tanks + water), roof height for chemical tanks, and sufficient ventilation for engine operation.

**Q3: Can a truckmount clean tile and grout?**
Yes — with a rotary tile cleaning tool or spinner head attached to the solution line. Truckmounts deliver the pressure and heat needed for effective hard floor cleaning. Ask CCW about compatible tile cleaning accessories.
