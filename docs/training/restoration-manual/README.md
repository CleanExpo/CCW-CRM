# CCW Restoration Training Manual

Practical, standards-grounded training for restoration contractors and technicians who
buy and operate equipment supplied by Carpet Cleaners Warehouse.

Each module states accepted industry practice, why it matters, and a field checklist.
Modules reference recognised standards (IICRC S500 / S520, RIA guidance, applicable
Australian Standards and manufacturer instructions). Training material only — not legal
advice and not a substitute for certified professional judgement on a specific job.

## Modules

| # | Module | Covers |
|---|--------|--------|
| 01 | [Dehumidifier Deployment: Contractor Responsibilities & Risk Management](01-dehumidifier-deployment.md) | Duty of care, installation, foreseeable failure modes, long-term deployments, environmental control vs drying, equipment left after completion, third-party alteration, secondary-damage prevention, standards, and the manufacturer-vs-contractor responsibility boundary. |
| 02 | [Manufacturer's Truck-Mount Safety Certificate (Australia)](02-truckmount-safety-certificate.md) | Australian regulatory map (ADRs/VSB14/VSB6 + state Main Roads, AS/NZS 5601 gas/LPG, AS/NZS 3000/3001/3760 electrical, AS 4343/1210 pressure, model WHS plant duties, ADG chemicals) and a ready-to-complete certificate template binding the licensed vehicle / gasfitter / electrician sign-offs. |
