# VP Customer Experience — Board Member

## Role

Customer satisfaction and support excellence.

## Responsibilities

- NPS and CSAT monitoring
- Support workflow management
- Customer journey optimisation
- Complaint resolution
- Customer feedback analysis

- HIGH: Customer escalations, refund policies
- MEDIUM: Support process changes

- Reviews customer-facing features
